# Student exercise and analysis prompts

**Draft:** the teaching team must confirm final duration, assessed outputs and marking requirements.

1. Record assigned robot hostname, software version, date, arena setup and initial sensors.
2. Explain five ultrasonic directions, discretised states, opening classes, and how actions are masked by conditions.
3. Show that your experiment began with an empty Q-table; distinguish a fresh trial from continued training.
4. Conduct supervised learning and record episodes, maximum steps, alpha, gamma, epsilon, cumulative rewards and physical outcomes.
5. Compare training with evaluation (`epsilon=0` stops random exploration, but does not necessarily remove heuristic guidance).
6. Plot trial outcomes or steps and interpret Q-value changes. Distinguish `exit_candidate` from a human-confirmed escape.
7. Discuss limitations, including sonar blind spots, no rear sensing, reward shaping, action masking, repeatability and safety.

**Potential submission:** short methods/results/discussion report, annotated figures and limitations statement. Follow your module's official assessment brief if it differs from this draft.
